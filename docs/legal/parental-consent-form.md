# Cat-ch — Parental / Guardian Consent Form (for children under 13)

> **Version 1.0 — adopted (pre-launch).** This is the verifiable-parental-consent
> (VPC) notice and form Cat-ch uses before a child under 13 uses the core game. It
> implements COPPA §312.4–§312.5 direct-notice + consent and supports GDPR-K Art. 8
> parental authorisation. Self-prepared by the operator; professional review
> recommended before public launch. The **verification method** in §4 must be
> finalised (and any `[PLACEHOLDER]` filled) before it is used with real families.

## Why you're seeing this

Your child has asked to play **Cat-ch**, a cozy game where players photograph real
cats and care for cute virtual companions. Because your child is **under 13**, US
law (COPPA) — and similar rules in the EU and UK — require us to tell you what we
collect and to get **your consent first**. Please read this and give or decline
consent below.

## 1. Who we are

Cat-ch is operated by `[LEGAL ENTITY / OPERATOR NAME]`. Questions or requests:
**nykefleym@gmail.com**. Full details are in our
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## 2. What we collect from your child, and why

| We collect | Why | What happens to it |
|------------|-----|--------------------|
| **A photo your child takes of a cat** | To recognise a real cat and create a cartoon companion sprite | Sent once to our image-generation provider to make the sprite, then **deleted immediately.** We do **not** keep the photo or use it to identify anyone. |
| **A coarse location** (rough neighbourhood, not precise) | To show a rough "where you met" label and a simple map | We **never** collect or store precise GPS coordinates. |
| **An age band and optional nickname** | To keep the experience safe and age-appropriate | Stored with the game profile until the account is deleted. |
| **Basic gameplay data** (companions, care) | To save your child's progress | Stored until the account is deleted. |

**We do NOT:** collect your child's real name or birth date, show your child's photos
to other users, build advertising profiles, use open chat, or allow your child under
13 to use any social/friends feature.

## 3. What your child can and cannot do

- **Can:** meet and care for companions, decorate, and learn responsible cat care —
  a safe, single-player experience.
- **Cannot (under 13):** add friends, trade, enter contests, visit others, share
  albums, or join clubs. These social features are switched off for children.

## 5. Your rights as a parent/guardian

At any time you may:

- **Review** the personal information we hold about your child.
- **Delete** your child's information and account.
- **Refuse** further collection or use of your child's information (this may mean the
  child can no longer use features that need it).

To do any of these, email **nykefleym@gmail.com**. We will verify your request comes
from the child's parent/guardian before we act.
